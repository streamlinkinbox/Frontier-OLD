SobolDirections.h contains generated numerical constants from the first 32
Joe-Kuo Sobol dimensions distributed with SciPy 1.15.3. No SciPy runtime or
third-party binary is vendored or required by the native C++ targets.

Direction numbers:
S. Joe and F. Y. Kuo, Constructing Sobol Sequences with Better Two-Dimensional
Projections, SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing 30(5), 2635-2654, 2008.
Search criterion 6; https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~fkuo/sobol/

Generation input: scipy/stats/_sobol_direction_numbers.npz, SciPy 1.15.3.
SHA-256: 4859931147d42ce465b8605cb277f957d98b839d03194fdf06579357906d193b
Comparison: scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol(d=32, scramble=False, bits=32)._sv
SciPy source: https://github.com/scipy/scipy/tree/v1.15.3/scipy/stats
